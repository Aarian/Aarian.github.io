<p>Welcome to my home page 😊 </p> 
<p> My name is Arian (Persian: <b>آریان </b>) – you can also call me <b>Ari</b>. 
 I am a Ph.D. student and research assistant in Departement of Electrical and Computer Engineering at <a href="https://www.concordia.ca/">Concordia University</a>.
 I received my M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Robotics) from the Department of Computer Engineering, <a href="https://aut.ac.ir/en">Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic)</a>. I also received my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering (Computer Hardware) from the CSE & IT Department of <a href="https://shirazu.ac.ir/en/home">Shiraz University</a>. I find great fulfillment in exploring the intersection of theoretical principles in machine and deep learning with their diverse applications. My master's research centered on statistical signal processing, specifically modeling space-time-frequency representations in image processing. I explored image decomposition techniques to uncover latent representations across new domains, coupled with the statistical analysis of the resulting coefficients. In terms of <b>Methodology</b>, my previous work was grounded in <i>Applied Machine Learning</i> and <i>Deep Learning</i>. Currently, my research focuses on <b>statistical modeling</b>, <b>optimization</b>, and <b>reinforcement learning</b>. In terms of <b>Application</b>, I have worked across diverse domains, including <b>medical image processing</b>, <b>wireless communication</b>, and <b>time series analysis</b>.
</p>
